Make It Home — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Make It Home is a personal-safety app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We only share your information when you trigger an alert or miss a check-in.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to deliver a safety alert:

  • Precise location — shared with your chosen contacts during an active safety session.
  • Contacts you add — stored to build your "safety circle."
  • Audio/video — an emergency recording, captured only while you are recording.
  • Your name — shown in the alert so your contacts know who needs help.
  • Mobile phone numbers — of the contacts you add, used solely to send the safety messages described below.

How your information is shared

When you trigger an alert or miss a check-in, the contacts in your safety circle receive your name, a live-location link, and any recording you captured. That is the only time your information is shared, and it happens because you initiated it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share mobile numbers. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in, or consent information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to deliver the safety messages you initiate through Make It Home.

Text messaging (SMS/MMS)

Make It Home sends safety notifications by text message to the emergency contacts a user has added to their safety circle.

  • Message frequency varies and depends on user activity. You will only receive messages when a Make It Home user who has added you triggers a safety alert, misses a check-in, or confirms they are safe.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help.
  • Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing, as stated above.

Security & retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Emergency recordings are automatically deleted within approximately 24 hours. You can delete all of your data at any time from the app's Settings screen.

Your choices

You can remove contacts, stop a session, or delete all your data from within the app. Message recipients can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
